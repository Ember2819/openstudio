# Code of Conduct

OpenStudio is a collaborative project for people who love making music and software. We want everyone to feel
welcome, whatever their experience, background, identity or tools.

## Our standards

Please:
- be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- keep discussion focused on the project and be patient with newcomers;
- respect other people's time, privacy and work.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others'
private information, and sexualised language or imagery.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to public spaces when
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people who
violate this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through a GitHub security advisory
or the contact details on the maintainers' profiles; reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
