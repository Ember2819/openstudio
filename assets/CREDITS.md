# Audio assets

## GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 — `soundfonts/GeneralUser-GS.sf2`
Not stored in git: `npm run assets` downloads it from a pinned commit of
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS and verifies its SHA-256.

By S. Christian Collins (https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser).
261 instrument presets and 13 drum kits (including TR-808/909, Electronic, Dance, Jazz, Brush and Orchestral kits).
Free to use without restriction for private and commercial music creation and to redistribute with software;
see `soundfonts/GeneralUser-GS-LICENSE.txt`. The author notes that some samples were sourced from freely
available banks of the early 2000s and cannot guarantee the provenance of every sample — if you plan to ship
OpenStudio commercially, read the license and consider replacing the bank.

## Built-in synth, effects and metronome
Generated in code (Web Audio); no third-party samples.

## MP3 encoder tables — `src/audio/mp3tables.js`
The ISO 11172-3 Layer III Huffman tables and synthesis window, generated by `scripts/gen-mp3-tables.mjs` from
PDMP3 (https://github.com/technosaurus/PDMP3), which is public domain (Unlicense). The encoder itself
(`src/audio/mp3.js`) is OpenStudio's own.
